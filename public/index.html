About

Hi, I'm Janell. I've been cleaning homes in the East Nashville area for over 10 years. I do the work myself, with occasional help when needed, so you'll always know who's coming and I'll know your place.

I do regular home cleaning and Airbnb turnovers, so whether you want someone weekly, monthly, or just a one-off clean, I can help. Every home and client is different, so I'm flexible about working around what you specifically need.

Services

Three ways I usually work — but if you need something in between, just ask.

Most common

Standard Clean

Dusting, vacuuming, sweeping and mopping floors, kitchen counters (outside of cabinets) and exterior of appliances, bathroom sanitizing (toilet, shower, sink), mirrors and glass, tidying, trash removal. Weekly, biweekly, or monthly schedules available.

Top to bottom

Deep Clean

Start with a standard clean and add on whatever detail work you'd like: baseboards, light switches and door handles, inside oven/fridge/microwave, inside cabinets, grout and soap scum, ceiling fans, window tracks, blinds, behind and under furniture, etc. Let me know what you'd like included and I can often accommodate.

For hosts

Airbnb / Short-Term Rental Turnover

Fast, thorough turnovers between guests — beds made, bathrooms spotless, kitchen reset, ready for your next check-in.
